Getting started

Holdwell needs only a few taps a day. This page shows what to log, when, and where to find each part of the app. It takes about five minutes to read.

What Holdwell does

Holdwell is a personal log for the time during and after a weight-loss medication. From a few quick entries it learns your own patterns, and lets you know when they start to shift, often before the scale does. It does not give medical advice, suggest doses or judge your values.

The first five minutes

  • Choose your phase: I'm losing weight on medication, I'm maintaining on medication, I'm tapering my dose or I've stopped my medication. It is only a label; you can change it any time in Settings.
  • Allow Apple Health so Holdwell can read and save your weight.
  • Allow notifications so short questions can come at the right moment.
  • The first time a timer appears on your Lock Screen, iOS asks whether Holdwell may show Live Activities. Choose Always Allow, otherwise its buttons will not work.

Every day: two taps per meal

  • When you finish eating, tap I ate on the Today screen. A timer starts and also appears on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • When you feel hungry again, tap I'm hungry, in the app, in the Dynamic Island or on the Lock Screen. The time in between is how long you stayed full. On a locked iPhone, iOS first asks you to unlock with Face ID or your passcode.
  • Nothing is measured after I'm hungry; the next I ate starts a new timer.

Every evening: one appetite score

Under How's your appetite? pick a score from 1 (none) to 5 (a lot) for the day overall. Once a day is enough; tap again if you change your mind. The evening question can remind you.

When something comes up: symptoms

Tap + Symptom when you notice nausea, constipation, tiredness, a headache or reflux, and mark it as passed when it does. Holdwell may ask later whether it has passed.

On injection day

  • Tap + Injection and save the date. The dose field is free text, written exactly as your doctor set it.
  • The first time, Holdwell asks Want a reminder for your next injection? Choose In 1 week, Pick a date or No, thanks, and whether to be reminded the day before, on the day, or both. The date is always your choice.
  • After that, each injection you log moves the next date along. You can change or turn off the reminder in Settings → Reminders.

Weight

Every weigh-in that reaches Apple Health, including smart scales, appears automatically. You can also tap Add weight. Holdwell uses the average of your last few weigh-ins, so a single day does not throw things off.

Radar: your own early warnings

First enter the weight you want to keep, your reference weight. Then add rules in your own words with Add rule. There is one rule of each kind:

  • Weight average: my average goes a set amount above my reference.
  • Weight rise: my average rises more than a set amount within a number of days.
  • Appetite streak: my appetite is at a set score or higher several days in a row.
  • Symptom streak: a chosen symptom is logged several days in a row.
  • Satiety: after stopping the medication, I stay full noticeably shorter than I did while on it. This appears only in the stopped phase.

When a rule is met you get a calm notification. Holdwell does not interpret the result; you may want to talk to your doctor.

Cycle: your week at a glance

Cycle shows how your fullness, appetite and symptoms change on each day after an injection. It needs at least two injection cycles of entries before patterns appear, so an empty screen in the first weeks is normal. From here you can also create an Experience Card to share your own experience without any medication name.

Shortcuts

  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island: the running timer with an I'm hungry button.
  • Home Screen widget: I ate and appetite buttons without opening the app.
  • Control Center and the Action button: one tap for I ate / I'm hungry. Settings → Quick logging explains how to add them.

If you stop the medication

Change your phase to I've stopped my medication in Settings. Injection reminders stop, and Holdwell starts comparing your fullness and appetite with your medication period.

Reminders

Settings → Reminders lets you choose which questions you get, how many per day, quiet hours, your injection reminder and a note after a few days without a weigh-in.

For your doctor

Settings → Summary for your doctor creates a PDF of your weight trend and entries to bring to an appointment.

Your privacy

There is no account and no server. Your entries stay on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. To erase everything, use Settings → Delete all my records. See the Privacy Policy.
